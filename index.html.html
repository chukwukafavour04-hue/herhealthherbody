Screening guide

    Monthly self-check

    Breast

    Look in a mirror, then feel each breast and underarm using flat fingers in small circles. Note lumps, dimpling, nipple changes or discharge.

    Skin

    Check moles for asymmetry, uneven borders, multiple colours, size over 6 mm, or any change over time.

    Body

    Notice bloating that does not settle, unexplained weight loss, ongoing fatigue, or bleeding outside your usual cycle.
